#PREAMBLE / LISCENSE

Hi! I'm BeeTheThird, and this repository is the home of my amateur indie game projects. All of the micro-games in this repo are made using Godot 4.7+ and using exclusively GDscript. This repository is under the MIT license; feel free to use any and all of the code herein if it's helpful to you! 

#PROJECT OUTLINES & ROADMAP

01: Table Tennis (Status: In Progress)

  A simple recreation of the classic game, Pong. Two white paddles on a black background, bouncing a ball between them. 

02: Wyrmling (Status: Not Started)

  A recreation of the classic Nokia game, Snake. The snake eats fruit(?), growing one segement longer each time. Chase your high score while avoiding your own snake-y body!

03: Jumpy Jetpack (Status: Not Started)

  A clone of the hit mobile game, Flappy Bird. Infinitely scrolling with randomly generated pipes and increasing speed / difficulty. 

04: Comet Commando (Status: Not Started)

  A clone of the classic arcade game, Asteroids. Buzz around in outspace blasting asteroids - without getting hit!

05: Plumber Bros. (Status: Not Started)

  A single screen, arcade style platformer a la Mario Bros 1983. Only a couple types of enemies, limited power ups. Wrap-around at the edges of the level.
  
06: Jump-man (Status: Not Started)

  A small-scale platformer a la Super Mario 3 or World. Maybe two or three levels. Basic power-ups included.

07: Dungeoneer (Status: Not Started)

  My attempt to make a top-down Zelda-like dungeon crawl. Puzzles and boss fight included. Will be relatively short. No Turtle Rock here.

08: Card Shark (Status: Not Started) 

  Some kind of deck builder. Maybe something like Slay the Spire, but much more condensed. 

09: Monster Arena (Status: Not Started)

  A miniature version of a creature-collector like Pokémon. My main goals will be to design the random encounter and battle systems. Mostly a proof of concept, NOT a full game (not even close).

10: Capstone Project (Status: Not Started) 

  TBD. I will decide on this at a later date. It will be a synthesis of the skills I have learned up to this point. Hopefully a feature-complete indie game of some sort. Small in scope but larger than any of the previous    projects.
